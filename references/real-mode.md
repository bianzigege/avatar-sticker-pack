# Human-Proportion Mode

Use this mode when the user asks for a human version, realistic version, adult version, or a non-Q sticker.

## Style lock

```text
Human-proportion hand-drawn sticker version of the supplied avatar. Preserve the original face, hairstyle, clothing silhouette, signature accessory, palette, and rendering language. Use natural adult body proportions and restrained expression. Simplify for a sticker without turning the character into a photo, glossy 3D avatar, generic anime protagonist, mascot, or chibi character.
```

## Composition

- Use a square canvas.
- Keep the whole recognizable head, shoulders or body, hands, and signature accessory inside the safe area.
- Let the pose communicate the intent before the text is read.
- Use a restrained white die-cut outline.
- Keep background transparent and contextual props minimal.

## Do not do

- Do not enlarge the head or shorten the limbs as a default.
- Do not beautify the face beyond the supplied avatar.
- Do not replace the original rendering language with photorealism or 3D.
- Do not turn “human version” into a photograph unless the user explicitly asks for a photo-based output.
