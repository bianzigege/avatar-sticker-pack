# Q Mode

Use this mode when the user asks for Q version, chibi version, cartoon stickers, or a more exaggerated reaction pack.

## Style lock

```text
Q-version hand-drawn sticker based on the supplied avatar. Preserve the same face cues, hairstyle, clothing silhouette, signature accessory, palette, and rendering language. Use a consistent 2.5–3 head proportion, slightly oversized head, compact body, clear hands, and stronger readable expression. Keep the character warm and recognizable, not baby-like, plush, glossy 3D, or a generic cute mascot.
```

## Composition

- Use a square canvas.
- Let the head, hands, signature accessory, and main prop form one clear silhouette.
- Exaggerate gesture and emotion, not identity features.
- Use a restrained white die-cut outline.
- Keep background transparent; use only a few motion marks or status symbols.

## Do not do

- Do not invent a new mascot unrelated to the avatar.
- Do not change hairstyle, signature accessory, or clothing identity.
- Do not mix different head-to-body ratios within the Q set.
- Do not use tiny props or dense scenes that disappear at chat size.
